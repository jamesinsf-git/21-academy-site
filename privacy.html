PRIVACY
POLICY

Last updated: July 2026

21 Academy is a blackjack training app for entertainment and educational purposes only. No real money is involved. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

◆

Your data stays on your device

No accounts, no name, no email, no location, and no advertising. We don't track you, and we don't sell or share data with anyone.

What we don't collect

21 Academy does not require an account and does not collect personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, or location, and we do not track you across apps or websites. There is no advertising in 21 Academy, and we do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data that stays on your device

Your training progress, session history, statistics, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and it is removed if you delete the app.

No real money

21 Academy is strictly a training and simulation app. All chips, bankrolls, and wagers are virtual and have no monetary value. The app does not facilitate real-money gambling of any kind and offers nothing to wager or win.

In-app purchases

21 Academy is free to use, with an optional one-time 21 Academy Pro purchase that unlocks advanced features. A free, time-limited Full Access Pass is also available and requires no purchase. All transactions are processed by Apple or Google through the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to verify purchase status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated identifier — no personal information — solely to confirm whether a purchase has been made and to restore it across your devices. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, your device may send anonymized crash diagnostics to Apple or Google, depending on your device-level settings. On iOS you can manage this under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements; on Android, under Settings → Google → Usage & diagnostics. We do not run any analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.

Children's privacy

21 Academy is intended for users aged 17 and older, in accordance with app store guidelines for apps that simulate gambling. The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date. Checking this page is the best way to stay informed.
